Epic 1:
    Energy & Stamina:
        Rework the current resource system into two separate mechanics: Stamina and Heat.

        Stamina:
        Stamina is the short-term activity resource used to start actions in activities.
        Every activity costs Stamina.
        Stamina represents physical and mental endurance.
        Stamina regenerates over time and can also be restored by rest, consumables, or special class effects.
        If the player does not have enough Stamina, the activity cannot be started.

        Heat:
        Heat is a pressure and exhaustion mechanic that affects risk and performance.
        Heat does not block actions directly, but it makes success harder.
        Heat starts at 0 and rises from risky actions, repeated activity runs, failed actions, special items, or specific locations.
        Heat is reduced slowly over time, by resting, by safe actions, or by items that reduce pressure.

        Core interaction:
        Stamina answers: how much can the player do right now?
        Heat answers: how dangerous or unstable is the player state right now?

        Gameplay rules:
        Activities always cost Stamina.
        Some activities also add Heat.
        Heat applies a negative modifier to activity rolls or increases the success target.
        High Heat should create tension without making the game feel unfair.
        Stamina should limit action volume, while Heat should shape action quality and risk.

        Suggested Heat thresholds:
        0-19 Heat: no penalty.
        20-39 Heat: -1 roll modifier.
        40-59 Heat: -2 roll modifier.
        60-79 Heat: -3 roll modifier and increased fail severity.
        80-100 Heat: -4 roll modifier and chance for extra consequences on failure.

        Design goal:
        The player should manage two different pressures.
        Low Stamina forces pacing.
        High Heat increases danger and reduces reliability.
        Together they create better decision-making, more tactical item usage, and clearer long-term gameplay depth.
